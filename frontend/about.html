About Us

University of Nottingham Ningbo China

The University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC) is the first Sino-foreign university to open its doors in China with the full approval of the Chinese Ministry of Education. Established in 2004, it is a pioneer in international higher education in China.

Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering

The Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering (CEE) at UNNC is committed to excellence in research and education. Our department focuses on developing sustainable solutions for chemical processes and environmental challenges.

Professor Sun Yong

Professor Sun leads our research team in the field of flavor chemistry and analysis. With extensive experience in food science and chemical engineering, Professor Sun's work focuses on understanding and characterizing flavor compounds in various food systems.
